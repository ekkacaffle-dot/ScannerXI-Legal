1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Caffle Scanner (“Caffle Scanner,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the Caffle Scanner app for iPhone. Caffle Scanner is a document scanning and productivity app that lets you scan documents, recognize text, read QR codes and barcodes, and organize, share, and print your files.

Caffle Scanner is designed so that your documents stay under your control. Scanning and text recognition are designed to run on your device, and we do not operate our own cloud server that receives your scans. Some optional features rely on Apple services or, in the case of product barcode lookup, a third-party service. Those cases are described below.

2. Documents and Scans

Document scanning is performed on your device. Caffle Scanner does not upload your scans, images, or PDFs to a server operated by us, and we do not have access to the contents of your documents.

Your documents are stored locally on your device or in a location you choose, such as iCloud Drive or another location available through the Files app. When you store documents in iCloud Drive or a third-party file provider, those documents are handled by that provider under its own privacy terms.

3. Camera Access

Caffle Scanner asks for permission to use your camera so that you can scan documents, QR codes, barcodes, and identification documents. Camera images are processed on your device to produce your scans or read codes. We do not receive your camera feed.

You can allow or deny camera access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera. Without camera access, scanning features will not work, but you can still import existing images and files.

4. Photos and Files

You can import images and documents into Caffle Scanner using Apple system interfaces such as the Photos picker and the Files document picker. Caffle Scanner only receives the specific items you select. Imported items are processed on your device and stored where you choose.

If you choose to save a scan to your photo library, iOS may ask you to allow Caffle Scanner to add items to Photos.

5. OCR

Optical character recognition (OCR), which turns text in your scans into selectable and searchable text, is designed to run on your device. Caffle Scanner does not send your documents to our servers for text recognition.

6. App Lock, PIN and Face ID

Caffle Scanner includes an optional App Lock that you can protect with Face ID (or another biometric method supported by your device) and a PIN.

  • Face ID: biometric authentication is handled by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Caffle Scanner only receives a success or failure result. Your Face ID data is not received or stored by Caffle Scanner.
  • PIN: your App Lock PIN is not stored in plain text. To verify your PIN, Caffle Scanner stores a salted SHA-256 hash in the Apple Keychain on your device. The Keychain item is protected by your device's iOS security.

Your PIN and its hash are not sent to us.

7. QR Codes and Barcodes

Caffle Scanner can read QR codes and barcodes using your camera or images you select. Decoding happens on your device. If a QR code contains a link and you choose to open it, the destination website handles your visit under its own privacy practices. QR codes are not looked up online. Product barcode lookup is described in the next section.

8. Open Food Facts

When Caffle Scanner reads a supported retail product barcode (such as a UPC, EAN, or GTIN code), it automatically looks up the product in Open Food Facts, an independent product database, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. You do not need to tap anything for the lookup to start. QR codes and other kinds of barcodes are not looked up.

What is sent

  • The barcode number. The same product can be printed with different but equivalent barcode formats, so Caffle Scanner may send several equivalent forms of the scanned number, one after another, until a match is found.
  • An app identifier. As Open Food Facts asks, each request names the app and its version (for example, “CaffleScanner/1.0”).
  • Standard connection information. As with any internet request, your IP address, the time of the request, and standard web request information are visible to Open Food Facts.
  • Product image request. If a product is found and has a photo, Caffle Scanner loads that image directly from Open Food Facts, which is a separate request to Open Food Facts' servers.

Caffle Scanner does not send your documents, scans, name, account, device identifiers, or location as part of these requests, and uses no cookies for them.

Server logs at Open Food Facts

Like most websites, Open Food Facts keeps server logs. These ordinarily record the requested address (which contains the barcode number) together with the IP address, time, and app identifier of the request. Open Food Facts' privacy policy states that it keeps visitor IP addresses and logs for up to three years for security, technical analysis, and popularity measurement, and that it does not share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. Open Food Facts is operated independently of Caffle Scanner, and its own privacy policy applies to the information it receives.

Results kept on your device

To avoid repeating lookups, Caffle Scanner keeps a small cache of recent lookup results on your device. It holds the product details that Open Food Facts returned, such as name, brand, size, and image address, for up to 7 days, and “not found” results for up to 24 hours. It holds at most 64 entries, does not include scans or images of your documents, and is never sent to us. iOS may also clear this cache at any time.

Search Web

If Open Food Facts has no entry for a product, Caffle Scanner offers a Search Web button. Nothing is searched unless you tap it. If you do, your default browser opens a Google search for the barcode number (for example, “UPC 012345678905 product”). That search is handled by your browser and Google under their own privacy policies.

9. iCloud and Apple Services

Caffle Scanner relies on Apple platform features, which may include iCloud Drive, the Files app, the Keychain, StoreKit, AirPrint, and the iOS share sheet. When you use these features, information is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and your device and iCloud settings. Caffle Scanner does not receive your Apple Account password.

10. Purchases

Caffle Scanner offers Scanner Pro as a one-time, lifetime in-app purchase. It is not a subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Caffle Scanner does not receive your payment card information or your Apple Account details.

To unlock Pro features, the app checks Apple's verified purchase record on your device. Caffle Scanner does not send purchase information to us or to any other party. You can restore a previous purchase at any time using Restore Purchase in the app.

11. Sharing and Printing

When you choose to share, export, or print a document, Caffle Scanner hands it to the destination you select through Apple system services, including AirPrint, Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Photos, and other apps available in the share sheet. After you share a document, the destination app, service, or recipient controls that copy, and their own privacy practices apply.

12. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

  • The current version of Caffle Scanner does not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The current version of Caffle Scanner does not include third-party behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • Caffle Scanner does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Apple may provide app developers with aggregated, anonymized information such as crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iOS settings. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

13. Data Retention

Because your documents are stored on your device or in a location you choose, you decide how long they are kept. Deleting a document in the app, or deleting it from iCloud Drive or another storage location, removes it from that location. This is subject to that location's own behavior, such as a “Recently Deleted” folder or backups.

Deleting Caffle Scanner removes the data stored in the app's local container. Keychain items, such as your App Lock PIN hash, may remain on your device after the app is deleted, as is standard iOS Keychain behavior. The on-device product lookup cache keeps results for up to 7 days (24 hours for “not found”) and is removed with the app. Information you have sent to third parties, such as a barcode lookup to Open Food Facts or a document you shared, is retained under those parties' policies (see section 8).

14. Children's Privacy

Caffle Scanner is a general productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate a server that receives your documents, we do not hold copies of content scanned in the app.

15. Security

Caffle Scanner uses the security features built into iOS, including app sandboxing, the Keychain, LocalAuthentication, and HTTPS for network requests to Open Food Facts. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Caffle Scanner detects password-protected PDFs and does not open them. It does not include its own PDF decryption.

To protect your documents, keep your device's operating system up to date, use a device passcode, and enable App Lock if you want an additional layer of protection within the app.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Caffle Scanner changes, for example if features are added or removed. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as through the app or its App Store listing.

17. Contact / Support

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Caffle Scanner handles information, please visit our Support page.
