1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ScannerXI (“ScannerXI,” “the app,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use the ScannerXI app for iPhone. ScannerXI is a document scanning and productivity app that lets you scan documents, recognize text, read QR codes and barcodes, and organize, share, and print your files.

ScannerXI is designed so that your documents stay under your control. Scanning and text recognition are designed to run on your device, and we do not operate our own cloud server that receives your scans. Some optional features rely on Apple services or, in the case of product barcode lookup, a third-party service. Those cases are described below.

2. Documents and Scans

Document scanning is performed on your device. ScannerXI does not upload your scans, images, or PDFs to a server operated by us, and we do not have access to the contents of your documents.

Your documents are stored locally on your device or in a location you choose, such as iCloud Drive or another location available through the Files app. When you store documents in iCloud Drive or a third-party file provider, those documents are handled by that provider under its own privacy terms.

3. Camera Access

ScannerXI asks for permission to use your camera so that you can scan documents, QR codes, barcodes, and identification documents. Camera images are processed on your device to produce your scans or read codes. We do not receive your camera feed.

You can allow or deny camera access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera. Without camera access, scanning features will not work, but you can still import existing images and files.

4. Photos and Files

You can import images and documents into ScannerXI using Apple system interfaces such as the Photos picker and the Files document picker. ScannerXI only receives the specific items you select. Imported items are processed on your device and stored where you choose.

If you choose to save a scan to your photo library, iOS may ask you to allow ScannerXI to add items to Photos.

5. OCR

Optical character recognition (OCR), which turns text in your scans into selectable and searchable text, is designed to run on your device. ScannerXI does not send your documents to our servers for text recognition.

6. App Lock, PIN and Face ID

ScannerXI includes an optional App Lock that you can protect with Face ID (or another biometric method supported by your device) and a PIN.

  • Face ID: biometric authentication is handled by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. ScannerXI only receives a success or failure result. Your Face ID data is not received or stored by ScannerXI.
  • PIN: your App Lock PIN is not stored in plain text. To verify your PIN, ScannerXI stores a salted SHA-256 hash in the Apple Keychain on your device. The Keychain item is protected by your device's iOS security.

Your PIN and its hash are not sent to us.

7. QR Codes and Barcodes

ScannerXI can read QR codes and barcodes using your camera or images you select. Decoding happens on your device. If a QR code contains a link and you choose to open it, the destination website handles your visit under its own privacy practices. QR codes are not looked up online. Product barcode lookup is described in the next section.

8. Open Food Facts

When ScannerXI scans a supported retail product barcode, such as a UPC, EAN, or GTIN code, it may automatically check Open Food Facts, an independent product database, to find product information.

The lookup happens over an encrypted HTTPS connection and the result is shown inside ScannerXI. ScannerXI does not automatically open the Open Food Facts website. The website opens only if you choose to tap an Open Food Facts link.

QR codes and unsupported barcode types are not sent to Open Food Facts.

What is sent

  • Barcode number: The scanned product barcode is sent to Open Food Facts to search for a matching product.
  • App information: The request may include ScannerXI's name and app version.
  • Standard network information: Open Food Facts may receive information normally associated with an internet request, such as your IP address, request time, and basic request information.
  • Product images: If a matching product has an image, ScannerXI may load that image from Open Food Facts.

ScannerXI does not intentionally send your documents, scanned pages, name, account information, precise location, or other personal files to Open Food Facts as part of this lookup.

9. iCloud and Apple Services

ScannerXI relies on Apple platform features, which may include iCloud Drive, the Files app, the Keychain, AirPrint, and the iOS share sheet. When you use these features, information is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and your device and iCloud settings. ScannerXI does not receive your Apple Account password.

10. Sharing and Printing

When you choose to share, export, or print a document, ScannerXI hands it to the destination you select through Apple system services, including AirPrint, Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Photos, and other apps available in the share sheet. After you share a document, the destination app, service, or recipient controls that copy, and their own privacy practices apply.

11. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

  • The current version of ScannerXI does not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The current version of ScannerXI does not include third-party behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • ScannerXI does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Apple may provide app developers with aggregated, anonymized information such as crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share analytics with developers in your iOS settings. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

12. Data Retention

Because your documents are stored on your device or in a location you choose, you decide how long they are kept. Deleting a document in the app, or deleting it from iCloud Drive or another storage location, removes it from that location. This is subject to that location's own behavior, such as a “Recently Deleted” folder or backups.

Deleting ScannerXI removes the data stored in the app's local container. Keychain items, such as your App Lock PIN hash, may remain on your device after the app is deleted, as is standard iOS Keychain behavior. The on-device product lookup cache keeps results for up to 7 days (24 hours for “not found”) and is removed with the app. Information you have sent to third parties, such as a barcode lookup to Open Food Facts or a document you shared, is retained under those parties' policies (see section 8).

13. Children's Privacy

ScannerXI is a general productivity app and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate a server that receives your documents, we do not hold copies of content scanned in the app.

14. Security

ScannerXI uses the security features built into iOS, including app sandboxing, the Keychain, LocalAuthentication, and HTTPS for network requests to Open Food Facts. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

ScannerXI detects password-protected PDFs and does not open them. It does not include its own PDF decryption.

To protect your documents, keep your device's operating system up to date, use a device passcode, and enable App Lock if you want an additional layer of protection within the app.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ScannerXI changes, for example if features are added or removed. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as through the app or its App Store listing.
