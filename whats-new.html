What's New

New features and improvements for ScannerXI.

Version 1.2 · Upcoming Update

Smarter OCR and More PDF Tools

We're working on new ways to convert, sign, and scan documents with ScannerXI. Here's what's planned for Version 1.2:

  • PDF to Word: Convert PDFs into editable Word documents, with improvements to text and layout preservation.
  • Sign PDF: Add your signature directly to PDF documents.
  • QR Wi-Fi Join: Scan a Wi-Fi QR code and use your device's native prompt to join a wireless network.
  • Advanced OCR: Improved recognition of tables, multilingual text, numbers, and multi-column or mixed-content documents.

Version 1.2 is in development and is not yet available on the App Store. Features may change before release.

Version 1.1 · Available on the App Store

Introducing Business Card Scanner

ScannerXI 1.1 makes it easier to capture and organize business card information.

  • Scan: Scan a business card or import a photo.
  • Recognize: Extract names, companies, phone numbers, emails, and addresses.
  • Edit and save: Check the extracted details, correct mistakes, and save the card.
  • Organize: Find saved business cards when you need them.
  • Improvements: Bug fixes and performance and stability enhancements.

Also in Version 1.1

  • Compact Quick Actions: The six Home shortcuts now fit in a 3 × 2 grid, so more of your documents are visible.
  • Redesigned Tools: All ten tools in a compact three-column grid, with Business Card in the last row.
  • Five recent documents: Home shows your five newest documents. See All still opens every file.
  • Tables in Text (OCR): Tables in scanned pages and photos are shown as rows and columns, even when the photo is tilted. Copy Table pastes them into a spreadsheet.

Version 1.1 is available on the App Store. Download ScannerXI or update the app to enjoy Business Card Scanner and the improvements above.
