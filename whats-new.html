Version 1.1 · Upcoming Update

Introducing Business Card Scanner

ScannerXI 1.1 makes it easier to capture and organize business card information.

  • Scan: Scan a business card or import a photo.
  • Recognize: Extract names, companies, phone numbers, emails, and addresses.
  • Edit and save: Check the extracted details, correct mistakes, and save the card.
  • Organize: Find saved business cards when you need them.
  • Improvements: Bug fixes and performance and stability enhancements.

Also in Version 1.1

  • Compact Quick Actions: The six Home shortcuts now fit in a 3 × 2 grid, so more of your documents are visible.
  • Redesigned Tools: All ten tools in a compact three-column grid, with Business Card in the last row.
  • Five recent documents: Home shows your five newest documents. See All still opens every file.
  • Tables in Text (OCR): Tables in scanned pages and photos are shown as rows and columns, even when the photo is tilted. Copy Table pastes them into a spreadsheet.

Version 1.1 is being prepared for App Store release. Availability depends on Apple's review and release process.
