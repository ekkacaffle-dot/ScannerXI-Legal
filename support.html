Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a document?

Start a new scan in ScannerXI and allow camera access if iOS asks. Place the document on a flat, well-lit surface with good contrast against the background, and hold your iPhone steady above it. Review each captured page and its edges before saving.

If scanning does not start, check that camera access is turned on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.

How do I scan multiple pages into one document?

After capturing the first page, keep scanning the next pages before you finish. All captured pages are saved together as one document. You can review the pages before saving.

How does OCR (text recognition) work?

OCR turns the text in your scans into text you can select, copy, and search. It is designed to run on your device, so your documents are not sent to our servers for recognition.

For the best results, use even lighting, avoid shadows and glare, and keep the page flat and in focus. OCR can make mistakes, especially with handwriting, small print, or unusual fonts, so check important text against the original.

Where are my documents stored? Can I use iCloud?

Your documents are stored locally on your iPhone or in a location you choose, such as iCloud Drive or another location in the Files app. ScannerXI does not upload your scans to its own server.

Documents stored in iCloud Drive sync through your Apple Account according to your iCloud settings. We recommend keeping backups of important documents.

How do App Lock and Face ID work?

App Lock protects ScannerXI with Face ID and a PIN. Face ID is handled by Apple. ScannerXI only learns whether authentication succeeded and never receives your biometric data. Your PIN is not stored in plain text. It is verified using a salted hash kept in the Apple Keychain on your device.

If Face ID isn't offered, check that Face ID is set up on your iPhone and that ScannerXI is allowed to use it in Settings › Face ID & Passcode or in ScannerXI's settings in the iOS Settings app.

How do QR code and barcode scanning work?

Point your camera at a QR code or barcode, or choose an image that contains one. Codes are decoded on your device. Check links before opening them, because QR codes can point to misleading websites.

When you scan a retail product barcode (UPC/EAN), ScannerXI automatically looks up the product on Open Food Facts over HTTPS, sending the barcode number. Product data comes from a third-party database and may be incomplete or outdated. If no product is found, you can tap Search Web to search for the barcode in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I print with AirPrint?

Open a document, tap Share, and choose Print. Make sure your iPhone and printer are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the printer supports AirPrint. If your printer doesn't appear, restart the printer and check its Wi-Fi connection.

How do I share or export as PDF, JPG, or PNG?

Open a document and use the share or export option to choose a format: PDF for multi-page documents, or JPG or PNG for images. You can then send it with the iOS share sheet to Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Photos, or other apps.

Once you share a document, the recipient app or person controls that copy.

Why can't I open a password-protected PDF?

ScannerXI detects password-protected (encrypted) PDFs and does not open them. It does not include its own PDF decryption. To use the document, remove the password with the app or tool that created it, if you are authorized to do so, and then import it again.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The ScannerXI version, if you know it.
  • What you were trying to do, step by step.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Whether the problem happens every time or only sometimes.

Screenshots can help, but never include screenshots that show document contents, IDs, passwords, PINs, payment details, or other personal information.
