Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan and save a business card?

Open Tools › Business Card in ScannerXI. Use the camera to capture the card, review the recognized details, correct anything that needs changing, and save it.

Can I import a business card from Photos?

Yes. In Tools › Business Card, choose the photo import option and select an image of the card. Review and edit the extracted information before saving.

What information can Business Card recognize?

ScannerXI can recognize details such as a person's name, company, phone number, email address, and address. If the card includes location details, review the street, city, state, postal code, and country fields when available.

Some fields may be missing or misread depending on the layout, language, print size, and image quality.

Can I correct business card details after scanning?

Yes. Review the recognized fields and edit any incorrect or missing information before saving. You can also open a saved business card to make corrections.

How do I find a saved business card?

Open the Business Card feature and use its search option to find a saved card.

How do I mark a business card as a favorite?

Tap the star icon on a business card to mark it as a favorite. You can use favorites to keep important cards easy to find.

Does scanning a business card automatically add it to iPhone Contacts?

No. Scanning saves the business card in ScannerXI; it does not automatically create an entry in Apple Contacts.

Why are business card details missing or incorrect?

Small or decorative fonts, glare, shadows, blur, and unusual layouts can affect text recognition. Try placing the card flat in bright, even lighting and taking a sharp, straight-on photo.

Always compare important contact details with the original card and correct any recognition errors before relying on them.

Where are my saved business cards stored?

Saved business card details remain on your device in ScannerXI. They are not automatically uploaded to a ScannerXI server or added to your iPhone Contacts.

What if the camera does not work when scanning a business card?

Check camera access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera on your iPhone, and make sure ScannerXI is allowed to use the camera. Then reopen ScannerXI and try again. You can also import an existing photo of the card.

If the problem continues, use the Email Support link and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and ScannerXI version. Do not send private card details unless necessary.

Is ScannerXI completely free?

Yes. ScannerXI is free to download and use. All current features are available without an in-app purchase or a subscription.

How do I scan a document?

Start a new scan in ScannerXI and allow camera access if iOS asks. Place the document on a flat, well-lit surface with good contrast against the background, and hold your iPhone steady above it. Review each captured page and its edges before saving.

If scanning does not start, check that camera access is turned on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.

How do I scan multiple pages into one document?

After capturing the first page, keep scanning the next pages before you finish. All captured pages are saved together as one document. You can review the pages before saving.

How does OCR (text recognition) work?

OCR turns the text in your scans into text you can select, copy, and search. It is designed to run on your device, so your documents are not sent to our servers for recognition.

For the best results, use even lighting, avoid shadows and glare, and keep the page flat and in focus. OCR can make mistakes, especially with handwriting, small print, or unusual fonts, so check important text against the original.

Where are my documents stored? Can I use iCloud?

Your documents are stored locally on your iPhone or in a location you choose, such as iCloud Drive or another location in the Files app. ScannerXI does not upload your scans to its own server.

Documents stored in iCloud Drive sync through your Apple Account according to your iCloud settings. We recommend keeping backups of important documents.

How do App Lock and Face ID work?

App Lock protects ScannerXI with Face ID and a PIN. Face ID is handled by Apple. ScannerXI only learns whether authentication succeeded and never receives your biometric data. Your PIN is not stored in plain text. It is verified using a value derived with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and a random salt, kept in the Apple Keychain on your device.

If Face ID isn't offered, check that Face ID is set up on your iPhone and that ScannerXI is allowed to use it in Settings › Face ID & Passcode or in ScannerXI's settings in the iOS Settings app.

How do QR code and barcode scanning work?

Point your camera at a QR code or barcode, or choose an image that contains one. Codes are decoded on your device. Check links before opening them, because QR codes can point to misleading websites.

When you scan a retail product barcode (UPC/EAN), ScannerXI automatically looks up the product on Open Food Facts over HTTPS, sending the barcode number. Product data comes from a third-party database and may be incomplete or outdated. If no product is found, you can tap Search Web to search for the barcode in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I join Wi-Fi by scanning a QR code?

Coming in ScannerXI Version 1.2. Wi-Fi joining is part of the upcoming update and may not be available in the current App Store version.

  1. Open QR & Barcode in ScannerXI.
  2. Point the camera at a Wi-Fi QR code, or import an image of the code from Photos.
  3. Tap the yellow Join prompt when ScannerXI recognizes the network.
  4. Review the network name and confirm Join in the iPhone's system prompt.

Your iPhone handles the connection. Scanning a Wi-Fi QR code does not connect you automatically without your confirmation.

What if a Wi-Fi QR code does not connect?

For the upcoming Version 1.2 Wi-Fi Join feature: Make sure the QR code is clear and includes a valid network name and password (if required). Move the camera away and scan the code again if you dismissed the Join prompt. Check that the network is in range and that its password is correct.

If the iPhone system prompt appears but the connection fails, try joining the network from iPhone Settings › Wi-Fi. Do not share Wi-Fi passwords or screenshots containing them in support messages.

How do I print with AirPrint?

Open a document, tap Share, and choose Print. Make sure your iPhone and printer are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the printer supports AirPrint. If your printer doesn't appear, restart the printer and check its Wi-Fi connection.

How do I share or export as PDF, JPG, or PNG?

Open a document and use the share or export option to choose a format: PDF for multi-page documents, or JPG or PNG for images. You can then send it with the iOS share sheet to Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Photos, or other apps.

Once you share a document, the recipient app or person controls that copy.

Why can't I open a password-protected PDF?

ScannerXI detects password-protected (encrypted) PDFs and does not open them. It does not include its own PDF decryption. To use the document, remove the password with the app or tool that created it, if you are authorized to do so, and then import it again.

How do I convert a PDF to Excel?

Open Tools › PDF to Excel and choose a PDF from ScannerXI Files or the iOS Files app. ScannerXI analyzes the PDF on your device and looks for structured tables. Review the table preview, then tap Export Excel.

The Excel workbook (.xlsx) is saved to ScannerXI Files using your selected storage setting. Tap View File, then Open File to preview it, or Share to send it to another app. You can also find it later in ScannerXI’s Files tab.

Conversion works best with clear, structured tables. Some PDFs may show “No table detected,” and password-protected PDFs must be unlocked in another tool before conversion. Check numbers, text, and columns against the original before using the spreadsheet. The original PDF is not changed.

What happens if I forget my App Lock PIN?

On the App Lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? and complete the iOS authentication prompt using your device passcode or an available authentication method. Then create and confirm a new ScannerXI PIN.

Resetting your PIN this way does not delete your saved documents, and App Lock stays enabled. We cannot retrieve your old PIN or bypass device authentication for you. Avoid deleting the app to reset your PIN, because deleting the app removes documents stored in its local container.

How do I delete or recover a document?

Choose Delete from a document’s actions to move it to Trash. To recover it, open Settings › Trash, tap the document, and choose Restore. The restored document returns to your files.

To remove a document permanently, select it in Trash, choose Delete Permanently, and confirm. Permanent deletion cannot be undone in ScannerXI. If an iCloud document is still downloading, wait for the download to finish before restoring it.

Deleting a document in ScannerXI does not automatically delete copies you previously exported to Photos, Files, another app, or another person. Those copies must be deleted separately.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The ScannerXI version, if you know it.
  • What you were trying to do, step by step.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Whether the problem happens every time or only sometimes.

Screenshots can help, but never include screenshots that show document contents, IDs, passwords, PINs, payment details, or other personal information.
